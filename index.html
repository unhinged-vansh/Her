the origin of words

You made a writer out of me without ever asking me to write.

You simply came into my life, and suddenly, I had things worth saying — feelings too beautiful to keep quiet.

Before you, words were only words. Sentences were only sentences. And poetry was something I admired from a distance. Then there was you. And somehow, every thought began looking for a prettier way to reach you.

I wrote about your smile when I couldn't stop thinking about it. I wrote about your voice when I missed hearing it. I wrote about the little things you probably never realized I had memorized. You gave me a reason to reach for my pen when I had nothing to say. And in return, I turned you into poetry. Not because you needed to be made beautiful — but because you already were.

insomnia

I realized I didn't have insomnia.

I just had a lack of love. The quiet got louder when there was nobody to tell about the day. And sometimes closing my eyes felt less like sleep and more like uncertainty — what if, when I close them, there will be no you in it at all?

strength

I measured the wrong kind of strong.

I used to think strength looked like heavy lifts, long days, controlled emotions, and simply pushing through. Then I met someone who walks gently through other people's pain. Turns out, I had been measuring the wrong kind of strong all along.

Baby,

I write because you return to me like a soft light breaking through the dark. You were my rainbow in the night, a fleeting glow I never learned to hold.

I find you in the pages of my memories, in every silent corner of my thoughts. You drift into my mind without warning, like wind through an open window. In the garden of my solitude, I would leave a flower for you — a fragile bloom carrying your name.

Tell me… will you come find me when I am gone? Or will you reach for my hand again and lead me somewhere beyond this world, just as you once did?

I miss you in ways words cannot carry. But I believe one day soon, we will sit together again… and we will laugh like nothing was ever lost.

Yours always, Baby Boy

the honest question

Can I have another chance?

Not because a website can repair a relationship. It can't. Not because pretty words erase what hurt. They don't. I am asking because what we had mattered to me in a hundred small ways, and I still believe something worth loving deserves one honest question.

I don't wanna be your annoying ex that texts you all the time after the break up. I just wanna be your baby again. I wish we could just sit down together, forgive each other for our mistakes, and realise that we were just kids learning how to love.
